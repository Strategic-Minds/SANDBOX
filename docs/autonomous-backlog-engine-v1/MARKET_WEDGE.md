# Autonomous Backlog Engine V1 — First Market Wedge

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: Discovery decision, not a production claim

## Decision
Pilot with **commercial specialty concrete / epoxy / polished concrete contractors** while keeping the engine schema and orchestration cross-trade.

The wedge is not "estimating software." The sellable outcome is **verified profitable backlog generated**: find qualified projects, assemble bid-ready evidence/work, route final pricing/submission to human approval, then attribute awards and realized margin back to the platform.

## 2026 evidence considered
- AGC/Sage report severe construction hiring constraints and increasing AI use in estimating/preconstruction.
- BuildOps describes AI estimating as a way to increase bid capacity, reduce scope errors, and protect margins without proportional headcount growth.
- 2026 market scans show dense incumbent software in roofing, HVAC/service, electrical, painting, and generic multi-trade takeoff. This increases competitive friction for those as the first wedge.
- Multi-trade AI tools already cover generic takeoff. The Backlog Engine must differentiate upstream (opportunity acquisition/bid-no-bid) and downstream (award/margin attribution and learning), not merely duplicate takeoff.

## Why concrete / epoxy first
1. Plan/spec and finish-schedule workflows are structured enough for human-reviewable AI scope extraction and takeoff support.
2. Commercial opportunity discovery is fragmented across GC invitations, plan rooms, procurement sites, public projects, and browser-heavy portals, which makes CloudBrowser materially useful.
3. The competitive market is less saturated with vertically dominant AI bid products than roofing or HVAC/service software.
4. The underlying outcome graph generalizes cleanly: project -> scope -> quantities -> price -> bid -> award -> actual margin.
5. The system can later expand horizontally into flooring, painting/coatings, then MEP trades without changing the economic north star.

## What must be proven before claiming product-market fit
- At least 5 design-partner contractors.
- A minimum of 100 real opportunities observed with provenance.
- Bid/no-bid ranking compared with human decisions.
- At least 25 bid-ready packages reviewed by estimators.
- Measured estimator-time reduction.
- Submitted bids only after human approval.
- Award evidence linked to originating opportunity/bid.
- Realized margin evidence for won jobs when available.
- Customer ROI calculated from evidence, never simulated data presented as actual.

## Competitive boundary
Do not compete as "another AI takeoff tool." The differentiator is the closed money loop:
**discover -> qualify -> bid-ready -> approve -> outcome -> margin -> learn**, with browser execution, governance, receipts, and hourly recursive optimization.
